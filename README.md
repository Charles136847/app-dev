# My Favorite Movie Series

## Title: Back to the Future Part: 1, 2, and 3

**Genre:** Science Fiction, Adventure, Comedy

**Release Year:** 1985

**Description:**

Back to the Future is one of my favorite movies because it has an interesting story about time travel, adventure, and friendship. It follows Marty McFly, a teenager who accidentally travels back to 1955 using a time machine built by Doc Brown. While trying to find a way back to his own time, Marty must make sure that his parents still meet and fall in love so that his future will not change.
